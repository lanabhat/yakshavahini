Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Yakshavahini ("the app", "the site") is a free, publicly browsable catalog of Yakshagana (Kannada performing-art) resources — musical meter patterns (Mattukosha), books (Pustaka Kosha), and organizations (Sanghatana Kosha). This policy covers the Yakshavahini Android app and the website it wraps at yakshavahini.web.app.

Information we collect

Browsing and searching the catalog does not require creating an account, and we do not ask visitors for any personal information.

  • No accounts, no personal data. The public app has no sign-in. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal data from visitors.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers. The app does not use Google Analytics, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking or profiling tools.
  • Anonymous view counts. We keep a simple aggregate count of how many times each catalog entry has been viewed, used only to surface "most viewed" entries. This count is not tied to any individual visitor or device.
  • Standard hosting logs. Like any website, our hosting infrastructure (Google Firebase Hosting) automatically logs basic technical request data (e.g. IP address, browser type, timestamp) for security and reliability purposes, as is standard for all web traffic. We do not access or use these logs to identify individual visitors.

Offline / installed app behavior

The Android app is a lightweight wrapper around the website above. When installed, it may cache the app's own interface (logos, styling, code) on your device for faster loading — this is standard web-app caching and does not involve collecting or transmitting any personal data. Catalog content itself is always fetched live from our servers.

Content management (editors and admins only)

A separate, invite-only admin panel is used by a small number of volunteers to add and review catalog entries. Admin sign-in uses Google OAuth, and some content (documents, PDFs) may be linked from Google Drive. This applies only to internal contributors managing the catalog — it does not involve or affect visitors using the public app or website.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience reference catalog and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Since this page is hosted directly on our website, the current version is always the one at this URL.

Questions about this policy or how the app handles data? Contact us at yakshavahini@gmail.com.
